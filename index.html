Auction Playbook — 7 Rules
1

Lock In Kamindu + 1 WI Pacer

These are non-negotiable. Kamindu's bat+bowl combo and a WI pacer exploiting SL's fragile batting are your foundation. Spend up to 55% here.

2

All-Rounders Are King in Tests

Chase and Dhananjaya de Silva bat, bowl, and are captains. Double/triple point streams. Prioritize them over pure batters.

3

Stack WI Pace (2 of 3)

Alzarri, Shamar, Seales — try to own 2. SL collapsed vs pace in the warmup. Antigua rewards fast bowling. This is your series edge.

4

Don't Overpay for Pure Batters

Seam-friendly conditions mean this won't be a run-fest. Batters who also bowl (Kamindu, Chase, DDS) give you insurance if batting collapses happen.

5

Snag a Spinner Late & Cheap

Prabath or Warrican at a discount. Most rivals will chase pacers. Spinners come into play Days 3-5 and can be match-winners for pennies.

6

Da Silva Over Kusal at Keeper

Da Silva is in domestic form (996 runs) and catches behind a lethal WI pace trio. If he's cheaper than Kusal Mendis, snap him up.

7

Mine the Dark Horses Late

Lahiru Udara (601 runs in 5 FC innings) and Amir Jangoo (avg 68.50 in WI Champ) are absurd value at base price. If they play, they outperform their cost 10x.
